# DealDone-

> **সহজ বাংলা চুক্তি ও লেনদেনের ডিজিটাল লিখিত প্রমাণ**  
> *100% Offline-First Legal Agreement & Receipt Generator for Bangladesh*

---

## 📌 Overview

In Bangladesh, millions of daily commercial and personal transactions occur on verbal trust alone—selling a used bike, lending ৳30,000 to a colleague, paying a ৳10,000 flat booking advance (*বায়না*), or hiring a contractor. Formal court stamp papers (৳300) are expensive and socially awkward (*"আমাকে কি বিশ্বাস করেন না?"*), while pure verbal promises invite betrayal, fraud, and disputes.

**DealDone** turns awkward verbal promises into legally sound, timestamped, digitally signed Bengali agreements and receipts in under **60 seconds**, completely offline.

---

## ✨ Key Features

1. **6 Standardized Bangladeshi Agreement Templates:**
   * 🏍️ পুরাতন গাড়ি / পণ্য বিক্রয় চুক্তি (Used Vehicle & Gadget Sale)
   * 🤝 টাকা ধার ও পরিশোধের অঙ্গীকারনামা (Personal Loan / Karze Hasana Promissory)
   * 🏠 বাসাভাড়া বুকিং ও অগ্রিম বায়না রশিদ (Rental Flat Advance Booking)
   * 🔨 কাজের অগ্রিম ও চুক্তিপত্র (Contractor & Freelancer Advance)
   * 📜 সাধারণ পারস্পরিক অঙ্গীকারনামা (General Mutual Agreement)
   * ⚖️ গ্রাম্য সালিশ ও আপোষনামা (Rural Dispute Settlement & Compromise)

2. **Automated Bangla Currency Transformer (টাকা কথায়):**
   * Real-time deterministic conversion of numbers to written Bengali words (e.g., `৳২৫,৫০০` ➔ *"পঁচিশ হাজার পাঁচশত টাকা মাত্র"*).

3. **Dual-Party Vector Signature & Tipshoi Canvas:**
   * Smooth finger signature capture on screen with minimum 15-point legal threshold validation.
   * *টিপসই* (physical thumbprint) and document photo attachments for non-signing parties or elders.

4. **Tamper-Evident SHA-256 Seal & QR Code:**
   * Real-time cryptographic hashing conforming to Section 65B & 85A of the **Evidence (Amendment) Act 2022**.
   * Single-page A4 vector PDF layout rendered using pure Dart and offline embedded *Hind Siliguri* TrueType fonts without broken Bengali conjuncts (*যুক্তবর্ণ*).

5. **1-Tap WhatsApp Dual-Receipt Sharing:**
   * Pre-fills polite, non-confrontational Bengali messaging directly to the other party's WhatsApp along with the attached PDF.

6. **100% Offline-First & Private:**
   * Zero cloud dependencies, zero analytics, zero external servers.
   * Encrypted local vault (AES-256) for searching and referencing past agreements.

---

## 🏛️ Legal Grounding in Bangladesh

* **The Contract Act, 1872 (Section 10 & 14):** Agreements are valid contracts when made by free mutual consent for lawful consideration.
* **The Evidence (Amendment) Act, 2022 (Section 65B & 85A):** Recognition and admissibility of digital records, electronic hashes, and digital signatures in Bangladeshi courts.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Prerequisites
* [Flutter SDK](https://flutter.dev) (v3.10+)
* [Dart SDK](https://dart.dev) (v3.0+)

### Installation & Run
```bash
# Clone the repository
git clone https://github.com/1ittleBee/DealDone-.git
cd DealDone-

# Navigate to the Flutter app directory
cd bayna_chuktigari

# Fetch dependencies
flutter pub get

# Run tests
flutter test

# Run the app
flutter run
```
